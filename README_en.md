## ✍️ Why LocalProse?

Stop renting your intelligence to Cloud AI services. **LocalProse** is a full-featured writing studio that runs directly on **YOUR** machine:

- **🏠 Absolute Privacy (100% Local-First):** Your manuscripts, notes, and lore bible stay stored on your devices. No data passes through remote servers or trains Big Tech AI models.
- **♾️ Zero Subscriptions, Zero Tokens:** Reclaim the "right to rough drafts." Test as many rewrites or editorial analyses as you want on your own processor (CPU/GPU), with no credit counters or external censorship.
- **🔓 Free Right Out of the Box (Desktop & Mobile):** Write with no mandatory account creation and no time limit, then unlock advanced features anytime via a one-click 7-day Pro trial or a Lifetime Pro License.

## 🚀 What LocalProse 4.0 Brings

- **📚 4-Level Narrative Architecture:** Structure your novels and sagas into **Volumes, Story Arcs, Chapters, and Scenes** via drag-and-drop, insert customizable scene separators, and track progress across 5 statuses linked to per-volume progress bars.
- **🧠 4-Persona AI Editorial Committee & Rewriting Studio:** Submit a chapter, a story arc, or an entire volume to the cross-analysis of **Victor Trame** (structure), **Sigmund Plume** (psychology), **Maestro Tempo** (pacing), and **Atlas Lore** (world consistency). Enjoy a side-by-side rewriting studio with the *"Imitate my style"* option, a contextual Thesaurus, and **Interview Mode** to chat live with your characters.
- **🗺️ Enriched World-Building & Timeline:** Detailed lore sheets (Characters, Locations, Items, Groups), interactive relationship map (**Canvas**), dual-view **Timeline** (in-universe *Time* / *Narrative* reading order), **Idea Box** expanded up to 4 columns, and automatic recognition of first names and nicknames as you type.
- **🎙️ Offline Voice Dictation & OCR:** Dictate your chapters in real time at the speed of thought and transcribe handwritten notes with no internet connection.
- **📖 Version History, Merging & Per-Volume Formatting:** Local restore points with visual word-by-word comparison, collaborative project merging, professional print preview for a standalone volume or the complete manuscript (Pocket, A5, and 6x9 formats, front matter, table of contents, footnotes), and open formats (`DOCX`, `PDF`, `EPUB`, `Markdown .md`, `TXT`).
- **🔄 "Zero-Cloud" Mobile Continuity:** Write anywhere on iPhone and Android for free, and merge your projects with your computer via a simple QR Code scan over your private Wi-Fi network (LAN).
- **🌍 7 Full Languages:** Interface, AI writing assistant, and offline voice dictation available in **English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch**.

## 🛠️ Installation (Desktop)

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/alexpoulet/localprose-releases/releases/latest)** tab.
2. Download the installer matching your system (**6 GB of RAM** or higher recommended):
  - **Windows 10/11 (x64):** `.exe`
  - **macOS (Apple Silicon M1 and later):** `.dmg`
  - **Linux:** `.AppImage` (universal format)
3. Launch LocalProse:
  - The **Free Edition** works offline right out of the box, with no account required.
  - Activate the **Free 7-Day Pro Trial** or a **Pro License** anytime.
  - The software automatically detects your hardware configuration and downloads the required models once so everything runs **100% locally and offline** thereafter.
